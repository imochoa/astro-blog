## Context

A well-written manual is worth its weight in gold, and Neovim's is pretty good! Or at least, the sections I have
read have been well written thus far.

These days, I mostly find myself poking around with `:h` to figure out how to
configure and use new plugins. However, I was tempted to read it "in full" after
seeing that the great [TJ DeVries](https://www.youtube.com/@teej_dv) made a
video **reading it**:
“[I Read the Entire Neovim User Manual](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rT-fbLFOCy0).”

So far so good, but I was surprised to see that you cannot just find a PDF
version. Don't get me wrong, https://neovim.io/doc/ is pretty nice, but I much
prefer reading (and making annotations) on a PDF.

## Solution

I thought this would be a one-shot task for AI, although it turns out
that it was a bit more involved than expected.

I decided against uploading the PDF to this blog because it is 14 MB and ~1.5k
pages-yikes! However, that is because this PDF includes everything and the
